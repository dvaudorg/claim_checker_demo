### Changes that can alter your results

- **Reader 6 reads PDFs more cleanly: text a figure hides, publishing details at the foot of the first page, the
  title and running heads.** Some publishers (MDPI among them) make a figure by showing a window onto a whole draft
  page; reader 5 read the hidden rest of that page too, as dozens of one-line "figures" that broke the paragraphs
  around them in pieces. Reader 6 reads only what the figure shows, so it is one figure with its caption and the
  paragraphs are whole. The e-mail addresses, the DOI and the dates ("Received …", "Available online …") that Elsevier
  and others print at the foot of the first page, after the introduction has begun, are set aside as front matter
  instead of read as text, as is a paragraph that is only a link or a DOI. A journal's name printed larger than the
  title is no longer taken for the title, and a title over two lines is kept whole. A running head with the page
  number at the other end of its line is found on every page, not every other one, so it no longer reads as a heading
  that cuts a reference list or the end notes in two. Word files, web pages and text files read as before. **Can
  change results, for documents you move to it.** New documents are read by reader 6; PDFs already in a project keep
  their reading until you choose *Read with the new reader*.
