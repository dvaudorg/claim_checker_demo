### New

- **ENLENS tells you when work has finished.** Work you started that took more than 20 seconds (a check, documents
  being added, your local model answering the queue) sends a notification from your computer when it has finished,
  or stopped with a problem. So you can close the browser while a local model works, which leaves it more memory:
  ENLENS keeps working, and a click on the notification opens it again. By default it comes only when no ENLENS tab is
  the one you are looking at; Settings › Checking › *When work has finished* also offers *Always* and *Never*, and
  *Send a test notification*. On a Mac it comes from ENLENS itself (a small notifier app ENLENS makes once, in
  ~/Library/Application Support/ENLENS/Notifier), and the first one asks whether ENLENS may send notifications; on
  Windows it is a toast under ENLENS's name; on Linux it goes through notify-send. Does not change results.
